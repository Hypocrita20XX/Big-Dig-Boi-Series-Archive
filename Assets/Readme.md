# Logo Creation Process

Because I value completeness and because I couldn't find any information to do this, I wanted to go over how I made the logo. The information that I think is more interesting is how I managed to get an isolated third person dwarf picture mid-salute in-game.<br />

**Note: I only sort of know what I'm doing with Photoshop. So take what I say with a grain of salt, there are almost certainly better ways to do what I did.**

## Required Mods
- [CorwinK's](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/corwink) [Third Person Vanity](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/third-person-vanity)
- [Buckminsterfullerene's](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/buckminsterfullerene) [Better Time Control](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/better-time-control)
- [SipcoGamingGuild's](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/sipcogamingguild) [Spacerig 17 After Hours](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/spacerig-17-after-hours)

## The Process

The first mod is needed because at least for me, using [AssemblyStorm](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/assemblystorm) and [Beer's](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/beer32) [Sandbox Utilities](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/sandbox-utilities) to try to get a picture of a third person dwarf mid-salute resulted in a broken model when going into free-cam mode.<br />

I really wish that would have worked because [TJK3's](https://mod.io/g/drg/u/tjk3) [Green Screen Bedrock](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/green-screen-bedrock) is fantastic for getting isolated images (this is how I got the Lootbug picture for both of my Lootbug mods, which can be found [here](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/lionhearted-little-loot-bugs "Lionhearted Little Loot Bugs") and [here](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/lionhearted-little-loot-sans "Lionhearted Little Loot-Sans") *#ShamelessSelfPromotion*.<br />

Never-the-less, because of that issue, I had to get creative, which is where Spacerig 17 After Hours comes into play.<br />

*Side-note: This all could be made much simpler if you 1) have friends and 2) they have the game and 3) don't mind posing for your silly projects. I have no idea how Better Time Control works in multiplayer though, so getting the images you need could be tricky in that context.*<br />

Anyways, one method I like using to isolate an image in Photoshop is by using a mask. I'll take one image, duplicate it, then mess up its levels/brightness/contrast/colors until I essentially recreate a green/blue screen effect where the subject is really dark and the background is really light. Then I'll select the light bits, clean up the selection a bit, delete the background, and bam, rough isolated subject. I'll then select that subject and add a small feather to the selection to smooth out the edges.<br />
It's a bit messy, but the results are passable, and it could be done better by just painting your own mask. I don't have that kind of patience, so the aforementioned method works well enough when the image in question wasn't made with a green/blue screen.<br />

This is why Spacerig 17 After Hours was crucial to the process. It allowed me to take a screenshot of my dwarf where he and the surroundings are darker than usual, so I could use 2 images instead of 1, which makes things easier. The only real question at this point was where to take the picture.<br />

The answer is easy: the launch bay. It's got this neat blue wavy sort of thing going on, which more or less mimics a blue screen (and my dwarf is wearing mostly yellow, so no color conflicts). So I got my dwarf into position, got the camera just right, made sure that Better Time Control's time setting was at the absolute minimum, made sure the lighting in the space rig was default, hit the v key, then the key to slow down time, and then took a screenshot.<br />

![Dwarf Screenshot](/Assets/Refs/000_dwarfmain.jpg)

After that, I quickly set Spacerig 17 After Hours to the extra dark setting and took another screenshot.<br />

![Dwarf Mask Screenshot](/Assets/Refs/001_dwarfmask.jpg)

Doing this in quick succession netted me the above screenshots with the exact same pose in the exact same position: perfect for my jank masking process. It's worth mentioning that Better Time Control *also* slows down camera movement (at least when using the Third Person Vanity mod, no idea how it operates otherwise), which made this screenshot process so much easier.<br />

After a bit of fiddling, I got this acceptable result:

![Isolated Dwarf](/Assets/Refs/004_isolateddwarf.png)

The only other part of the puzzle that you see in the logo is [Bosco-Chan](https://mod.io/g/drg/m/bosco-chan-model-replacement-anime "Bosco-Chan by Hlomida and Fancyneer").<br />
That actually came about by accident. I was messing around in a mission figuring out if I could reasonably convey a hole when after making said hole, Bosco-Chan got in the frame in *exactly* the right way and I realized just in time to get the perfect screenshot:

![Bosco-Chan](/Assets/Refs/002_boscochan.jpg)

She even had this nice blue outline that makes the jank mask process a bit easier. Applying the same principles to her as I did the dwarf, I managed to get her isolated as well.<br />

A bit of color adjusting, outlines, a fair bit of editing, masking, and getting text to follow a curve [using this guide](https://www.bwillcreative.com/how-to-type-text-in-a-circle-in-photoshop/) then adding a vignette gave me a satisfactory result.<br />
